For Corporate Executives

You built someone else’s company. Who’s building your plan?

Strategic planning for executives whose wealth is tied up in stock, options, and deferred compensation. The package is sophisticated. The plan behind it usually isn’t — and the most expensive decisions are made at vesting and exercise, not at tax time.

See where you stand

Most executives aren’t ready for their equity.

Stock, options, and deferred comp decide how much of your wealth you keep — and most of those choices are made long before the shares vest. The Scorecard shows you where the gaps are, in about five minutes.

The problem no pay stub shows you

Your net worth may be one ticker, one vesting schedule, and one tax bracket away from a very different number.

Concentration

One company carries everything

Your salary, your bonus, your RSUs, your options, often your 401(k) — all tied to the same employer. A diversified employee can still be a very concentrated investor.

Tax

The bill is set before you see it

Vesting, exercise, sale, and deferral elections each create a tax moment. Handled one at a time, they can stack up — including AMT and bracket creep — faster than most people expect.

Timing

The windows open and close on their schedule

Trading windows, blackout periods, deferral deadlines, and expiration dates don’t wait for you to have a plan. The choices are made in advance, or they’re made for you.

Who we work with

Built for executives with real complexity — and a lot riding on one company.

  • Senior leaders and officers at public and private companies.
  • Significant wealth in company stock — RSUs, performance shares, stock options, or an ESPP.
  • Deferred compensation, supplemental retirement plans, or a package with moving parts you didn’t design.
  • Executives thinking about a transition — a new role, a change of control, or retirement — who want the plan in place first.

What this isn't

This isn’t a stock tip, a single product, or a one-time tax fix. It’s a custom plan built around your compensation, your family, and what you actually want your wealth to do. If you’re looking for someone to pick the next hot stock, we’re probably not the right fit — you already own too much of one.

One plan, every dimension

Your company gave you a compensation package. It didn’t give you a plan to go with it.

Equity compensation

RSUs, options, and ESPP shares coordinated into one strategy — when to hold, sell, exercise, or diversify.

Concentrated stock

Ways to reduce single-company exposure over time, including pre-arranged 10b5-1 trading plans where appropriate.

Tax strategy

Planning around vesting, exercise, AMT, and deferral elections — designed to reduce the drag on what you keep.

Deferred compensation

Deferral elections and distribution choices made with your whole plan in view, not in isolation.

Income & liability protection

Disability, life, and umbrella coverage around the income and assets your family depends on.

Estate & legacy

How your equity and wealth pass to the people you love — with less friction and less lost to taxes.

Each plan is custom, built around your circumstances and designed to help you reduce taxes, fees, and cost, protect more, spend more of what you’ve earned, and pass more to the people you love.
