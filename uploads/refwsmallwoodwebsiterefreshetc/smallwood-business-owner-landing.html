The problem no P&L shows you

Your net worth is one asset, one tax code, and one exit away from a very different number.

Concentration

One asset carries everything

Most of your wealth sits inside the business. That's efficient for building it and fragile for keeping it. A single event — a market, a buyer, a health issue — moves everything at once.

Tax drag

The cost you never see billed

Entity structure, timing, and how income and gains are recognized quietly decide how much you keep. Left on autopilot, tax drag compounds against you year after year.

Timing

The moves happen before the sale

By the time a term sheet is on the table, most of the options are gone. What you do in the years before the exit is what decides how much of it survives.
